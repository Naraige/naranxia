Dark veil, Knight of Darkness, Winter Feast: a night and a winter to survive.

## Add
- Name your king in your profile: his name is written everywhere in the game.
- Winter Feast, from 1 December to 6 January: snow all around, and gift bearers walk with the horde, rich in gold for whoever strikes them down.
- The first boss is now the Knight of Darkness: black armour, a great sword, and every light near him grows dim.
- Seasonal events follow the calendar: Spring Fair, Midsummer Fires and Night of the Horde come next.
- The co-op window wears the menu buttons and has Copy and Paste for the room code.
- The launcher and its shortcuts can take a new picture with an update (install the new launcher once).

## Change
- At night the unlit ground becomes a dark veil: your units cannot walk into it, except to reach a lit place. Bannermen and captains carry their light through it.
- Towers, walls and gates reach farther; units are drawn at full size; buildings accept rougher ground.
- The ranking lists every player in co-op.

## Fix
- Co-op convoys: a porter cut off from the road stays at work and the others carry his share.
