One island, one bridge, one King to walk and a dragon to ride: Naranxia as it was never played.

## Add
- Castle or Dragon: in co-op each player picks a role. One holds the castle, the other rides a dragon over the horde.
- You play your King: the game opens beside him, the keys walk him, left click strikes, right-drag turns the view. The build menu lifts the view from above.
- The dragon: 20 levels, 6 shapes, 9 ranks, a tree of 28 skills bought with gold, fireball, roar, looks and flame colours. It can be hurt, falls and comes back; the sanctuary is its roost.
- The King can mark the ground for the dragon: its fire burns harder there.
- A stone bridge is the only way onto the island, and the portals of the horde stand on the islet beyond it. A gate closes the bridge.
- The horde scouts you: kill its three scouts between waves or it comes by your least guarded road. From wave 4 a chieftain leads it.
- A button to call the next wave for gold, a summary after every wave, a peaceful mode without waves, a code to carry your dragon's progress to another machine.
- Butterflies, birds, falling leaves and fish: the land lives.

## Change
- The world is one large island with a natural shore and cliffs, and a wide flat heart with room to build; no more rival kingdoms.
- Paths lay themselves from every building to the castle. Units stand at the scale of the block buildings. Stairs are one block high.
- The fog of war hides everything, the shape of the land too. Menus and the top bar wear the design of the main menu.
- Peasants near their King work faster; towers on high ground shoot further; the watch beacon sees further.
- Effects: chips bounce, smoke billows, bursts throw a ring of light, damaged buildings smoke and burn.

## Fix
- The royal guards no longer stand inside the King and block him.
- No second castle in the choice of the site.
- The build menu no longer shows buildings of another category; the top bar shows large stocks whole.
