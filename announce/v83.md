**Naranxia v83 is out!**
- Continuous ramparts: walls, towers, gates and keeps join seamlessly, tower to tower and diagonal walls too.
- Build anywhere: no more build-zone limits.
- One Guild and one Academy per kingdom; foresters can't be placed where their planting areas would overlap.
- Destroyed buildings give the ground back its original height.
- Found your castle anywhere on land: only its own square is levelled, and a ramp out is guaranteed.
- Digging goes through dirt, then a stone layer (each stone block gives stone), down to unbreakable bedrock.
- Water flows into holes dug next to it.
- Idle zombies walk around trees and rocks instead of pushing into them.
- Save / Load in solo games, with an autosave at the start of every wave.
- New desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux: plays offline, keeps your saves, updates itself at launch.
