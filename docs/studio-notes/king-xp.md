# King XP and talents (hd-gameplay)
- King unit fields (saved whole, absent = level 1): `kxp` (xp), `kt` (talent bitmask), `kdr` (damage factor, recomputed each tick). XP: zombie dying within 10 tiles of a living King (+1, brute 3, acid/bomber 2), each wave survived (+3, from wave 2), boss slain (+12 to every King). Levels at 25/70/140/240 xp (level 5 max, +25 % hp heal on level up), one talent point per level above 1 (4 points, 8 talents, so one branch full or 2+2). Code: `gameplay/king-skills.js` (hooks: simulation.js death branch, waves.js `wave0`, statistics.js `kingTick(dt)`).
- Talents, learned in order inside a branch (command `kt`, wrapper `kingTalent`, no new SAVA key): Command: long cry (+4 s), wide cry (+5 tiles), fierce cry (x1.5 instead of x1.35), warlord (cd -11 s, units under the cry take half of Frost King chill and boss stomp). Combat: heavy charge (x1.5 dmg, 4 tiles), royal plate (-20 % damage taken, regen 0.8 hp/s), long charge (+2 s, cd -7 s), boss breaker (charge x2 on bosses and delays their power timer `bs` by 6 s).
- UI: second row under the skill pills in `kingBar` (level and xp, or one green button per branch while a point is free); end screen line "King Lv N  Talents M" from `GST.kl/kn` (absent-tolerant). Texts: docs/studio-texts/king-xp.json (15 keys, lead merges with addtexts).
- Lead must run: det mp8 mpk mp_resync save features kingxp mp_kxp (new: tests/kingxp.js, tests/mp_kxp.py, both in tools/test_all.mjs and the quick list). fitcheck for the new pill labels. No difficulty change; war cry buff now reads `u.wcM` (default 1.35).
