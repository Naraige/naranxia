# events-camps (hd-gameplay)

- Choice events: caravan (wave %4=2) and rebel envoys (wave %6=4) open a banner with two buttons (`ui/event-bar.js`, drawn under the top bars in `ui/hud.js`). Caravan: take goods now (old gift) or escort it (gold x2.5, paid 40 s later). Envoys: accept tribute or refuse (+1 renown = +10% on every rebel tribute, max 10 points). Constants at the top of `gameplay/events.js`. Meteor shower stays automatic.
- Lockstep: click -> `evChoose` -> command `{k:'ev',i,c}` (`multiplayer/commands.js`, `case'ev'` -> `resolveEvent0`); first command wins, a stale id is ignored. Deadline = 30 s of SIM time (`eventsTick` in `update0`), then choice 0 is taken. Two events on the same wave (wave 10, 22...) queue. State: `eventsState` (SAVA key `EVT`, fields tolerated when absent), renown in `RESP[p].ren`. No SVVER bump needed (new key, `reset()` clears it).
- Camp traits (`ai/kingdoms.js`): `baseTrait(b)` = pure hash of world seed and camp id (no RNG consumed, lazy for old saves, settlements inherit). merchants: envoy tribute x1.5; warlike: the group a wave sends against that camp is 25% tougher (`waves.js`); wary: the first envoy is turned away (`b.wrf`, envoy kept, 60 s cooldown). Shown as "Name - trait" in the wave message, tribute messages and the event title.
- Texts: `docs/studio-texts/events-camps.json` (20 keys, FR/ES/DE/IT/PT), to merge with `tools/addtexts.py`.
- Tests: `tests/events.js` (`npm test -- events`, in the quick suite) and `tests/mp_events.py` (`npm test -- mp_events`: two clients, first click wins, timeout, event open across a resync). `tests/envoy.js` now forces a neutral trait on its camp.
