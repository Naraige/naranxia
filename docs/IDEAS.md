# Naranxia: lore and content ideas (not built)

## Lore (simple version, chosen by the King)
Naranxia is the last bastion of humanity. The other kingdoms next door (the AI kingdoms) are rebel camps: survivors who refused the King's rule.
Zombies: the horde that ended the old world. Raids by rebels and convoys between camps tie into this.

## Content (no story mode, no clan challenges)
- Named seasonal bosses, random events (caravan, eclipse, wolves, meteors), ruins to explore with lore fragments, kingdom journal.
- New units: purifier priest, mounted scout, siege engineer, rebel envoy.
- New buildings: sanctuary, observatory, royal forge, watchtower beacon, granary, apothecary, market, embassy, war room, bell tower, sappers' workshop, orchard.
