# Changelog

All notable changes to Naranxia, newest first. Generated from the GitHub releases by `tools/changelog.py`.

## v97 (2026-11-22)

A kingdom of your own: profile, pattern banners and a world built in blocks.

- At the first launch you create your kingdom: its name, its two colours and the pattern of its banner. Change it at any time from "Your kingdom" on the title screen.
- Banners are made like Minecraft banners: 16 patterns, hung on gates, towers, the castle, the barracks and the war room, and carried by the bannerman.
- Every roof wears the colour of your kingdom.
- Co-op: each player keeps his own name, colours and banner on both screens, with the name of each kingdom over its castle. Players cannot hurt each other.
- Every building is redrawn in blocks, one block to a tile: buildings are much larger and more detailed.
- Walls, towers and gates are laid on a grid of four tiles: thick ramparts with a wall walk, great towers, a gate with a portcullis.
- Saves from older versions load without their old walls, towers and gates.

## v96 (2026-11-18)

Faster big hordes and a clearer Esc key.

- Big hordes cost much less processing: the game stays smoother with thousands of zombies.
- Esc now drops a selected building or the castle first, and opens the main menu on the next press.

## v95 (2026-11-17)

Spread-out hordes, body blocking and formation previews.

- Hordes spawn spread out and flow around crowded tiles: fewer pile-ups in the lanes.
- Units can no longer stand on top of each other: they push apart, and standing troops hold their ground.
- Defensive stance: troops strike what is in reach and never chase, then walk back to their post.
- Formations show green and red slot markers on the ground while you place or drag them.
- Tab still jumps to the last attack for a minute after the alert marker fades.
- Co-op: spikes have an owner, random starts stay on the main land, and a running game asks before it is replaced.
- Co-op: saved games remember which castle belongs to which player.
- Zombie lanes find routes over hills and mountains more often.

## v94 (2026-11-16)

Co-op fixes, waves that always end, and new default keys.

- Zombies no longer get stuck in water or on cliffs: every wave can now be finished.
- Co-op: after a resync both games now restart from exactly the same state, and a clear one-line message says what is happening while you wait.
- Co-op: if your partner leaves, you stay player 2 with your own castle. Saved games now continue exactly as the original game would.
- New default keys: torch F6, barracks rally point F7, forester F9, guild F3. On AZERTY the camera is now on Z Q S D.
- The minimap now frames the area you explored, and banners no longer cover the event buttons.
- Waves now appear far from your castle, so in co-op you no longer see your partner's wave spawn on top of you.
- Hordes no longer pile up in narrow passages, and big waves run smoother.
- Spikes now show a placement preview and can be demolished for a full refund.
- Zombie lanes find a way around rock and woods more often, so fewer hordes cross mountains or pile up on trees.

## v93 (2026-11-12)

Comfort and fixes after the big update.

- The Update page shows clean section titles and is translated in every language.
- Video guide: sounds in the clips, five new chapters (traps, seasons, market, rebel camps, King talents) and a Guide entry in the Esc menu.
- Rebel camps now field spearmen, more of them in warlike camps. The Spearman's default key is F4.
- Co-op convoys: porters no longer stop short of your partner's castle, long detours are allowed, and a convoy with no land route is refused at once.
- Co-op: the convoy quest is skipped when the two castles are not linked by land.
- AZERTY keyboards: the number row now works without Shift.
- The quest arrow stays clear of the top bars and points from the screen edge when the building is off screen.
- Faster horde simulation, and proofread Portuguese, German, Dutch, Polish and Turkish texts.

## v92 (2026-11-12)

The biggest Naranxia update so far: a new look for the whole game, a deeper last bastion, and a real welcome for new players.

**A whole new look**
- Every unit, building, weapon and tool rebuilt in a blocky pixel style, with team tabards, rank stripes and pixel health bars.
- The world is made of blocks: terrain, trees, rocks and seasons.
- Mutants are now true monstrosities, and wave bosses are bigger and meaner.
- New title screen, menus, launch intro, kingdom banners on a pole, and remade sounds and music.

**New to play**
- Quests guide your first game, from the first log to wave 10, with small rewards. They work solo and in co-op (shared with your partner), and a marker shows the button to press.
- New unit: the Spearman, a cheap line holder with reach, strong against brutes and bosses.
- The tutorial is now a video-style guide with short looping clips.
- Last bastion: ruins to loot, rebel camps with personalities, envoys and tribute, scouts, engineers and eleven new buildings.
- New events: wolf packs, eclipses and meteor showers. Caravans and rebel envoys now ask you to choose.
- Named bosses with legends and powers, a realm journal in the Chronicle.
- The King gains XP, levels up to 5 and learns talents in two branches.
- Research with either-or paths, and barter at the market.

**Comfort**
- Leaderboard with your top 10 right on the title screen, and an Update page for what changed.
- Colour-blind mode, touch controls, one-time hints, public co-op lobby, more languages.
- Saves always load after an update.

## v91 (2026-11-06)

- Seasons and weather: a season lasts 4 waves (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter), each wave has its own weather (clear, rain with thunder, fog, snow), with falling petals, pollen and leaves, season colours, weather sounds and a label in the top bar.
- Wave boss: every 10th wave a giant brute joins the horde with its own health bar. It stomps buildings and troops around it; killing it pays 400 gold.
- King skills: War cry [<] (your troops near him hit 35% harder for 8 s) and Royal charge [>] (he runs and hits much harder for 4 s and throws zombies back). Buttons under the top bar, keys can be changed in Options.
- Defense: spike traps (passable, zombies standing on them are hurt until they break; drag to place several), Boiling oil research (walls, gates and towers scald zombies next to them) and Iron age research (units hit 10% harder, buildings slowly mend).
- Co-op: ping types (Alt+click look, +Shift defend, +Ctrl gather, +Shift+Ctrl attack) and shared goals (reach wave 5, 10, 15... and slay zombies together) that pay both players.
- End of game: stats (resources earned, best unit, units lost), your three best runs on the same map and a "new record" banner, daily map flagged.
- Guided tutorial the first time you play (Esc menu > Tutorial to see it again), touch controls (pinch to zoom, two fingers to move, tap the ground to order), dust on hits and construction, a snowy bell in the winter calm music.

## v90 (2026-11-06)

- New look: every unit is now a Minecraft-style character with pixel textures: King Naraige (royal helm under his crown), captain, royal guard, knights, archers, peasants, bannerman and gunners.
- New look for the zombies: each one is still different (clothes, helmets, wounds), with greenish skin and glowing eyes; skeleton runners, the chained brute, the bomber with his powder keg, the acid spitter and the miner with his lamp.
- New weapons and shields in the Minecraft item style (shields based on "Minecraft Shield" by bugbilly, CC BY 4.0).
- New castle: a square fortress with chunky battlements, a keep with a steep slate roof and a gatehouse with a portcullis, in wood at the start and in stone after the upgrade.
- Capes, the bannerman's banner and the cannon redone in the same style; buildings get Minecraft block textures (stone bricks, planks).

## v89 (2026-11-06)

- New: Troops panel (F2 or the army button at the top): every unit by type and what it is doing. Click a line to select those units.
- New: peasant tasks. Select peasants, then Wood, Stone, Gold, Farm or Build sends them to the nearest job.
- New in co-op: resource convoys. Castle menu > Send: choose gold, wood, stone and food, and porters carry them to your partner's castle (dots on the map). Zombies can stop them; if they cannot get through in 2 minutes, the goods come back.
- New research: Fire arrows (Academy, after Fletching). Archers set zombies on fire for 3 seconds.
- New: bug report. Press F8 (or Esc > Report a bug) to save a file with everything needed to fix the bug, then send it on Discord with a few words.
- New: test mode for solo games (F10): resources, research, waves and zombies on demand, speed up to x8. A game where it is used earns no achievements.
- Unit names in the selection are now translated.

## v88 (2026-11-05)

- Co-op: when the two games drift apart they now resynchronize by themselves in a few seconds (the host sends its game to the partner): no more restarting or reconnecting. The banner shows "Co-op: resynchronizing...".
- Co-op: fixed a cause of drifting apart: zombies and AI units were given a different owner on each machine.

## v87 (2026-11-05)

- Turn your castle before founding it: press R (or "Turn castle") on the site screen; its door faces the side you choose.
- Castles now stand at the height of their site instead of sinking into hillsides.
- Co-op: you see your partner's castle (and where they are looking) while choosing your site, the two castles may stand side by side, and both players need the same version to start.
- Co-op: the second player's land is protected like the first one's (AI kingdoms no longer settle next to it, hordes are sized the same).
- Our units take ramps to change level: blocks you raise or dig can no longer be climbed (zombie miners still cut steps for the horde).
- Double-click a building to select all of yours of that type: Upgrade turns every wooden one to stone at once.
- Click a tower or your castle to see its range as a ring on the ground.
- New "Quit game" button in the menu (in co-op your partner carries on alone), and "Exit to desktop" in the desktop app.

## v86 (2026-11-01)

- Sharper icons everywhere: every icon is now drawn at 2.5x the resolution with a new painted finish (rim light, smooth outline, soft shadow).
- New look for menus and windows: rounded, bevelled panels and buttons with a glossy top, soft shadows and a glow on hover and selection.
- New desktop icon and launcher screen; fresh installers are attached to this release.
